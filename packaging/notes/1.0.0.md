The first stable McGyver: a file wheel for KDE Plasma 6, macOS and Windows. Drag files, hold Shift, and drop them on a wedge: pictures change format, videos shrink, PDFs turn into Word files, folders get zipped. Everything runs on your computer.

## New in 1.0.0

- The app checks once a day whether a newer version is out, and the tray menu and the settings offer it.
- The daily check also sends the app's version, the system and its version, the processor type, the language and a one-way hash of the machine's ID; the site keeps them with the IP address and the country. No file or file name ever leaves the computer. See https://mcgyver.ferdiunal.tr/#privacy; `MCGYVER_OFFLINE=1` keeps the app from asking at all.
- The version shows in the tray menu, in the settings' About, and in McGyver.exe's details on Windows.
- macOS: started from the disk image or Downloads, the app offers to move itself to the Applications folder.
- Windows: on PCs without a graphics driver (virtual machines, Remote Desktop) the app draws with Mesa's software OpenGL, which ships in the x64 zip. When it can't start, it says why and where its log is (`%APPDATA%\McGyver\McGyver.log`).

## First start

- **KDE Plasma:** press **Set up** in the settings' Permissions card once, so the wheel opens the moment you press Shift during a drag.
- **macOS:** the app isn't notarized yet. Control-click it → Open (from macOS 15 on: open it once, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway), and allow Input Monitoring when asked.
- **Windows:** the exe isn't signed yet; SmartScreen asks once: More info → Run anyway.
