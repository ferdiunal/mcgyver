Drag a file. Hold Shift. Pick a wedge.

McGyver opens a wheel around your pointer while you drag files. Drop them on a wedge: pictures change format, videos shrink, PDFs turn into Word files, folders get zipped. Conversions run on your computer, not in a cloud.

Version 1.0.0 · KDE Plasma 6, macOS and Windows

    A demo: nothing leaves this page, and no file is touched. Drag a file onto the space and hold Shift, or just click one.

    One wheel, seven modes

    The ring around the centre switches between them. Stop on a slice, or move along the ring into it.

    With files

    1. Convert

      The wheel opens on Convert. Aim at a format and let go: holiday.webp lands next to holiday.jpg. Pictures, video, audio, PDFs, Office files, archives and subtitles each get their own list.

    2. Tools

      Compress, trim, mute or rotate a video, or pull stills out of it. Pictures resize, crop and lose their EXIF; PDFs split and rotate, and two or more merge into one.

    3. Actions

      Zip it, copy or move it somewhere, copy its path, open a terminal there, or send it to your phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack or Teams.

    Without files

    Hold Shift on its own for a moment and the wheel is for the computer instead.

    1. Apps

      What is open, the one you used last on top, then the apps you picked in the settings. Alt+Tab, in a circle.

    2. Media

      Whatever is playing: play or pause, next, previous, repeat, shuffle, and the player itself.

    3. Everyday

      Keyboard layout, the computer’s language, volume, brightness, screenshots, screen recording, and how busy the computer is.

    4. System

      Lock, Dark Mode, Stay Awake, the microphone, the trash and sleep. Try Dark Mode: this page goes dark with it.

    Every file and folder also gets Actions: Zip, Copy, Cut, Copy To, Move To, Copy Path, Terminal and Share. Merge appears once two or more files are in hand. HEIC and AVIF appear where the computer’s libheif can write them.

    Get McGyver

    One download, then it waits in the tray or the menu bar until you hold Shift.

    KDE Plasma 6

    Linux, on Wayland. Where McGyver started.

    After the first start, press Set up in its settings, so the wheel opens the moment you press Shift during a drag.

    Download for Linux

    A .deb for Debian and Ubuntu, or the .rpm for Fedora.

    macOS

    macOS 11 or later. One app for Apple Silicon and Intel.

    Open the disk image and the app offers to move itself to Applications. It isn’t notarized yet: the first time, Control-click it and choose Open (from macOS 15 on, Open Anyway in Privacy & Security).

    Download for Mac

    Windows

    Windows 10 and 11, on x64 and ARM64 PCs.

    On a PC without a graphics driver, like a virtual machine, it draws with Mesa’s software OpenGL, which comes in the zip. SmartScreen asks once: More info, then Run anyway.

    Download for Windows

    For x64 PCs, or ARM64.

    Privacy

    Once a day the app asks mcgyver.ferdiunal.tr whether a new version is out. That request also carries the app version, the operating system and its version, the processor type, the app’s language, and a machine ID that is a one-way hash of the system’s own ID (it can’t be turned back into it). The server also sees the IP address and the country it comes from, and stores them with that ID.

    No files, file names, or what you do with the wheel ever leave the computer. To keep the app from asking at all, start it with MCGYVER_OFFLINE=1 in its environment.
