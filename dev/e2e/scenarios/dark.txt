# Dark Mode from the desk: with the look like the system's (the default), the open wheel turns
# with the desktop at once. System's wedges here: Lock, Dark Mode, Stay Awake, Empty Trash,
# Desktop, Sleep (no microphone: PipeWire is out of reach), clockwise from the top.
# It starts on Breeze's light colours; plasma-apply-colorscheme writes the nested session's own
# kdeglobals (out/config), so out/scheme-1.txt should read BreezeDark and scheme-2 BreezeLight.
log 1 shift held alone, to SYSTEM, onto Dark Mode
move 640 400
sleep 800
key 42 1
sleep 900
wiggle 300
glide 592 400 10 20
press
sleep 50
release
sleep 500
glide 742 341 10 20
wiggle 300
shot dark-1-before
log 2 a click switches it, and the wheel turns with it
press
sleep 50
release
sleep 1200
shot dark-2-switched
shell grep '^ColorScheme=' $OUT/config/kdeglobals > $OUT/scheme-1.txt
log 3 again, back
press
sleep 50
release
sleep 1200
shot dark-3-back
shell grep '^ColorScheme=' $OUT/config/kdeglobals > $OUT/scheme-2.txt
key 42 0
sleep 800
log done
